## Architecture delivered

`ResearchWorkspaceService` produces one immutable `ResearchSnapshot` per successful research request. The snapshot is the UI boundary for the workspace and contains:

- canonical symbol and market identity;
- source/provenance metadata, price freshness, price history, and indicator data;
- the existing canonical composite score and its coverage, never a second scoring formula;
- structured missing-data, limitation, warning, risk, and evidence records;
- an optional company profile and an optional scenario reference generated by existing research logic.

The Dashboard builds and stores one snapshot after a successful search. A later failed search does not replace the current usable snapshot. The Research Workspace renders the snapshot without recalculating provider, indicator, or score data in the UI layer.

## Research Workspace

The delivered workspace contains identity header, Overview, Chart, Fundamentals, Score explanation, Evidence and data, and Risk and scenarios tabs; a compact overview company context and one-year chart; full chart controls for 6M, 1Y, 3Y, and MAX with existing SMA20/SMA60 data; explicit partial, insufficient-data, stale, and error states; and the existing research-only scenario reference section, explicitly labelled as not investment advice.

### Fact and inference boundary

Company name, industry, sector, and public company-description fields are displayed as fact data only when supplied by a source. Business, technical themes, supply-chain links, applications, and bottlenecks are labelled as research inferences. When source facts are unavailable, the workspace states that company information is insufficient rather than filling a generic profile.

### Charts and limitations

The MVP renders OHLC candlesticks where required fields exist and falls back to a close-price line where they do not. It does not fabricate volume, intraday data, or future prices. It deliberately does not provide interactive drawing tools, real-time streaming, benchmark overlays, or a charting-terminal replacement.

### Evidence, score, and provenance behavior

Evidence items retain their kind (`fact`, `calculation`, or `research_inference`) and source metadata. Scores remain the existing `StockScoreResult`; coverage states how much of the required score input is present. An incomplete composite score appears as `資料不足`, not as a numerical estimate. Provider source type and resolved query symbol are shown when available.

## Known limitations

- This is a local, evidence-labelled research presentation, not external generative AI and not a source of buy/sell instructions.
- Online company and market data remains best-effort and may be delayed, incomplete, unavailable, or stale.
- Fundamental availability determines score coverage. The product intentionally reports partial or insufficient data instead of manufacturing a composite score.
- Charts are daily-data research charts, not real-time market data or a TradingView replacement.
- Snapshot persistence and a multi-snapshot research library are outside Sprint 5 scope.

## Rollback

The previous formal release is retained by the Sprint 4.1.2.2 baseline artifacts. The Dashboard also retains the existing Legacy Dashboard diagnostic path in Settings. A release rollback consists of re-deploying the accepted Sprint 4.1.2.2 artifact/source baseline; it must not delete or overwrite `%LOCALAPPDATA%\StockTool` user data.
